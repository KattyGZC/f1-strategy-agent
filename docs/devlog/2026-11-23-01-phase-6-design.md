# 2026-11-23 · 01. Phase 6: design of the assistant

**Goal:** settle the assistant's threat model, its API and tools, and every provider fact the design relies on, as ADRs, before any code.

## What happened

The session ran stage 2 of phase 6 with three skills from an external collection: security-and-hardening, api-and-interface-design and source-driven-development. The agent read the code the assistant builds on: the error shape, the SSE route of the replay, the edge, the settings and the clean model. In parallel, research subagents checked the provider documentation: Anthropic's spend-limit errors, streaming, citations, caching and pricing; Voyage's models and SDK; the FIA's editions and terms of use. The agent presented the threat model, then the contract, one decision at a time. The author settled each one, and the agent wrote [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-the-assistant-is-a-bounded-tool-loop-behind-one-port.md) to [ADR 0024](../adr/0024-regulations-by-edition-embedded-in-pgvector.md) and pointed the spec's open questions at them.

## Decisions

- Our own tool loop behind a `LanguageModel` port, with citations from the provider's search-result blocks checked against what the tools returned: [ADR 0021](../adr/0021-the-assistant-is-a-bounded-tool-loop-behind-one-port.md).
- Read-only tools in `READ ONLY` transactions, limits at every layer, and a budget of 2 USD a day within 25 USD a month, with the provider's spend limit as the hard cap behind it. Prose is rendered as paragraphs, bullets and bold only: [ADR 0022](../adr/0022-the-assistants-threat-model-and-budget.md).
- `GET /api/assistant` for the state and suggestions; `POST /api/answers`, following the existing plural-noun routes, answered as typed SSE events; refusals through a `decline_question` tool; citations open in a panel inside the tab: [ADR 0023](../adr/0023-the-assistants-api-and-tools.md).
- The regulations come from a hashed manifest, are searched in the edition in force at the race's date, are shown as a short excerpt with a link to the FIA's PDF, and are embedded with `voyage-4` called over HTTP: [ADR 0024](../adr/0024-regulations-by-edition-embedded-in-pgvector.md).
- New dependencies `anthropic` and `pypdf`, approved by the author.

## Corrections and redirections

- The agent proposed a daily budget of 2 USD. The author asked whether 25 USD had not already been agreed. The agent explained that the daily limit is a slice of the same 25, so one abusive day cannot pause the demo for the month. The author kept 2 USD.
- The agent proposed calling Voyage over HTTP instead of its SDK, which brings nine dependencies. The author asked whether LangChain or LangGraph, which job postings ask for, belonged here. The agent advised against both: the loop is about a hundred lines, a framework's types would cross every layer, and provider citations arrive late in frameworks. The author agreed. The alternatives are recorded in ADR 0021.
- The tech audit of the agent's own draft found that "the latest edition published before the race" would pick next season's regulations, since the FIA publishes them during the current season. ADR 0024 now chooses within the race's own season, with an explicit tie-break.
