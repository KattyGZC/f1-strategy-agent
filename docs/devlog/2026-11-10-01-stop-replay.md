# 2026-11-10 · 01. A viewer stops their replay

**Goal:** choosing another session stops the replay the viewer leaves and frees its place at once.

## What happened

1. The audit of the replay screen ([PR 11](https://github.com/KattyGZC/f1-strategy-agent/pull/11)) noted that leaving a replay kept it running until its idle expiry. The author asked to solve it, in its own PR from `main` once PR 11 was merged.
2. The agent read the replay store and the producer, proposed a stop route and asked about scope; the author chose to stop only on the button, not on closing the tab.
3. Built on `feat/stop-replay` in five commits by layer, with [ADR 0011](../adr/0011-a-viewer-stops-their-replay.md).

## Decisions

- **Stop on the button only**: [ADR 0011](../adr/0011-a-viewer-stops-their-replay.md). A request on closing the tab is not guaranteed to be sent, and a reload would stop a replay the viewer meant to keep.
- **A stop looks like an expiry**: the producer and the viewers end as they do at the idle expiry; an append to a gone replay returns that it appended nothing.
- **Fail loud on the screen**: a stop that fails, other than a replay already gone, is shown in the picker; the replay is left to its idle expiry.

## Corrections and redirections

- The agent proposed a separate PR stacked on PR 11. The author chose a PR from `main` after merging PR 11.
- The agent first said a replay gone during an append left an exception nobody awaited. Reading `update` again, it returns None for a gone replay, so the producer only ended as failed; the agent corrected the claim before building.
- The author asked what happens on closing and reopening the tab: the screen starts at the picker and the old replay expires after five idle minutes. Resuming it is a possible later change, not taken here.
